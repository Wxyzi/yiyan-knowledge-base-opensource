# 从研究兴趣到可检验的问题

“我对课堂反馈感兴趣”是研究兴趣，但还没有说明需要观察什么。这篇新编的简短示例用于展示知识笔记，不是完整的研究方法指南。

## 把兴趣写成问题

设想一个虚构任务：比较同一门课两次写作作业中的反馈处理情况。问题可以是：“学生在第二次提交时，处理了哪些类型的修改建议？”

问题中要能辨认出研究对象、情境和可观察的现象。此时不要预先写成“某种反馈一定提高成绩”，因为数据还没有支持这个判断。

## 让数据对应问题

可以记录反馈条目、反馈类型和后续修改情况。先规定怎样判断“已处理”，再检查不同判断者能否一致使用规则。不要把没有修改直接解释成“不愿意学习”，还可能存在理解困难或任务限制。

### 一个练习

把自己的兴趣写成一句问句，然后列出回答它至少需要的两类记录。如果记录无法支持结论，就调整问题，而不是扩大解释。

## 下一步：保留证据链

用[可追溯的证据笔记](/articles/sample-evidence-note?lang=zh)把原始材料、观察和解释分开保存。涉及真实学生时，应先完成适用的伦理审查和授权，并移除可识别身份的信息。

# From an Interest to a Research Question

“I am interested in classroom feedback” states an interest, but does not identify what to observe. This newly written sample demonstrates a knowledge note; it is not a complete research methods guide.

## Turn the interest into a question

Imagine a fictional classroom task comparing feedback handling across two submissions. A question could be: “Which types of revision requests do students address in the second submission?”

Identify the participants or materials, the setting, and an observable phenomenon. Do not assume that a feedback strategy improves scores before gathering evidence.

## Match evidence to the question

Record each feedback item, its category, and the corresponding revision. Define “addressed” before coding, and check whether different coders can apply the rule consistently. A missing revision does not necessarily indicate low motivation; misunderstanding or task constraints are alternative explanations.

### A short exercise

Write your interest as one question and list two kinds of records needed to answer it. If the records cannot support the conclusion, refine the question rather than expanding the claim.

## Next: preserve the evidence trail

Use a [traceable evidence note](/articles/sample-evidence-note?lang=en) to separate source material, observation, and interpretation. For real participants, complete applicable ethics and permission steps and remove identifying information.
